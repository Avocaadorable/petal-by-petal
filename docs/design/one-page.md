# Petal by Petal — one-page design

## Destination

“A cozy flower shop game with soft pinks, light greens, and layered floral arrangements.” The game is for players who enjoy calm creative customization, decorative aesthetics, and getting to know characters. It is a single-player desktop game for Windows and macOS, in 16:9 landscape at 1920 × 1080 and a 60 fps target on the owner's M4 Max test machine.

The first playable milestone is one complete customer commission. The defining experience is learning about a customer through natural, distinctive dialogue, then expressing that understanding in a flower arrangement. Completion requires the owner to play the whole commission and an independent screenshot and playtest review. There is no timer or fail screen.

## Player loop

1. A customer arrives with a request and a clearly visible budget.
2. The player has three short dialogue beats, each with two or three choices. Choices reveal personality, story, and flower preferences. Thoughtful responses may deepen the relationship or unlock story; no choice punishes the player or silently changes the order.
3. The request and discovered preferences remain visible while arranging. Optional tips explain how to satisfy them.
4. The player chooses flower types and colors, then drags stems from inventory into a vase in a fixed three-quarter view. They reposition stems and adjust height, rotation, and tilt with smooth mouse controls; clear rotation, click-to-remove, and undo controls are available. Prefer the left mouse button.
5. On delivery, gentle feedback and payment reflect how well flower, color, and style preferences were met. Tips may reward a strong match.

## First milestone scope

One shop, one original customer, one vase, and one flower developed end to end to prove the complete arranging workflow. The customer commission includes the conversation, visible request and budget, arrangement, delivery, and feedback. The shop is illustrated; vase arranging is a dedicated 3D editor. Build simple geometry and procedural assets in Godot. Use only owner-provided or appropriately licensed free assets.

## Later, out of scope for the first milestone

The market and sellers, purchasing and inventory growth, additional customers and longer stories, a broad flower/vase/material library, reputation, long-term shop growth and identity, and the full ending. Keep the future loop in view: visit a local market, buy flowers and materials, fulfill commissions, grow relationships and reputation, and shape the shop through arrangements.

## Design principles

- Calm and low-pressure: no countdown, fail screen, or punitive dialogue outcome.
- Customers matter: dialogue reveals who they are and makes their request legible.
- Creative freedom: arranging supports add, move, rotate, tilt, height adjustment, and remove.
- Clear feedback: request, preferences, budget, and optional guidance remain visible; delivery explains results kindly.
- Original identity: borrow interaction ideas from *Flowers and Favours: Florist Simulator*, while keeping Petal by Petal's characters, story, and design-bible look.

## Audio

Soft calm music, with optional quiet shop sounds such as footsteps, paper wrapping, scissors, water, and conversation. Silence should remain comfortable and focus-friendly.
