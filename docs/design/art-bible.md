# Petal by Petal — art bible

This transcription uses the owner's supplied `design-bible.pdf`, especially pages 1–3 and 7. The source is the visual authority; the art decisions below combine those notes with the owner's grill answers.

## Mood and style

Cozy, romantic, botanical, watercolor-inspired, soft, layered, and calm. Use recognizable flowers with simplified characters and environments. Keep shop, characters, and interface illustrated. The vase editor is 3D, with soft lighting and painterly flower textures that complement the illustrated world. Avoid gore, harsh neon, and stressful countdowns.

## Materials and shapes

Reference material vocabulary: rough burlap, fluted paper, soft felt, linen weave, satin, vellum, gold foil, and embossed leather. Use warm wood for floors and paneled walls. The source labels satin as “shint satin”; retained here as satin pending clarification. Layered floral compositions should remain legible and recognizable.

## Shop construction

Use small repeatable floor and wall sections with matching edges. Wooden floors connect to walls and entrances. Entrance steps remain below knee height. Walls are taller than characters. Counters, flower stalls, and arrangement tables are about waist-high. Place vases, baskets, plants, and signs on floors, tables, walls, or ceilings as appropriate.

## Camera and screen

Use a fixed three-quarter camera, pitched 30° downward with a 35° field of view. Use soft warm-neutral lighting around 4,500 K. Keep color brightness within roughly 15%–95%, and text and UI clearly readable. Layout is 16:9 landscape at 1920 × 1080, with a 60 fps target on the M4 Max test machine. Illustrated characters are approximately four heads tall.

The first procedural 3D flower has a maximum of 1,500 triangles and uses 512 × 512 painterly textures.

## Asset route and constraints

Build the shop, modular architecture, counters, table, vase, and simple first flower procedurally or with Godot geometry. Use owner assets or free assets with an appropriate license for other needs. Start with one flower and complete the full workflow before expanding the library. Total and per-session spending cap: $0. Do not run paid Flora generations.

## Exemplar

The owner-accepted exemplar is the design bible itself, including the soft-pink/light-green palette and watercolor direction. Preserve that visual direction in the shop and UI; reference-game screenshots inform interaction only.
